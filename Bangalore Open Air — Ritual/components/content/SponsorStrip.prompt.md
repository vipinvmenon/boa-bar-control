**SponsorStrip** — partner lockups on ink, equal optical weight.

```jsx
<SponsorStrip label="Presented with" sponsors={['BookMyShow','Wacken Foundation','Infinite Dreams','Old Monk','Paken']} />
{/* or real logos */}
<SponsorStrip logos={[{ src:'assets/bms.png', alt:'BookMyShow', height:24 }]} />
```

Text names desaturate/brighten logos to sit evenly on ink. Prefer real logos when available.
