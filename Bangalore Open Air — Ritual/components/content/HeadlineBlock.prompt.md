**HeadlineBlock** — eyebrow + condensed headline + subhead, the core ad message unit.

```jsx
<HeadlineBlock eyebrow="Phase 2 Now Live" headline="The Ritual Returns" subhead="India's longest-running outdoor metal festival." accent align="center" size="lg" />
```

`accent` turns the headline gold (headliner spotlight). `align`, `size` (sm/md/lg).
