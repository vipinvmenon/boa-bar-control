**CTAButton** — the festival call-to-action.

```jsx
<CTAButton variant="primary" size="lg">Get Tickets</CTAButton>
<CTAButton variant="ghost">See Lineup</CTAButton>
<CTAButton as="a" href="#" variant="green">Buy Now</CTAButton>
```

`primary` gold fill (reserve for the main action), `ghost` green outline, `green` poison fill. Sizes sm/md/lg. Presses with a 1px nudge.
