**TicketBadge** — a ticket-tier pill.

```jsx
<TicketBadge tier="Headbangers Pass" price="₹1,999" tone="green" />
<TicketBadge tier="Eddies Lounge" price="₹4,999" tone="gold" />
<TicketBadge tier="Early Bird" soldOut />
```

`tone`: green (standard), gold (premium), ghost (muted). `soldOut` strikes the tier and dims.
